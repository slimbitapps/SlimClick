## 🇬🇧 English

### Name
SlimClick – Presentation Remote

### One-sentence description (überall gleich verwenden!)
Free app for Windows and Mac that turns your phone into a presentation remote – scan a QR code, no app install.

### Short description (max. 160 Zeichen)
Scan a QR code and control PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides or PDF from your phone. Laser pointer, preview, timer. Free.

### Description
```
Giving a talk? Your phone is all the clicker you need.

Open SlimClick on your laptop. It shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera – a web page opens, and that's your remote. No app to install on the phone, no account, no cables.

WORKS WITH EVERY PRESENTATION APP
SlimClick presses the same keys as a real clicker, so it works with PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, LibreOffice Impress, Canva and PDF viewers – on Windows and Mac.

MORE THAN NEXT AND BACK
• Big Next button – or just swipe on the preview
• Live slide preview on your phone – see what the audience sees
• Laser pointer: touch the preview and a red dot appears exactly there on the projector
• Spotlight: only one spot stays bright
• Go to slide number, black screen, start and end the presentation
• Presentation timer (countdown or stopwatch): the phone screen flashes 5, 2 and 1 minute before the end
• Landscape mode and left-handed layout

WORKS ON SCHOOL WI-FI
Many school and company networks keep devices apart. SlimClick notices and shows a step-by-step guide: turn on your phone's hotspot, connect the laptop, scan again. The connection uses no mobile data.

SECURE
The QR code contains a one-time key that is never sent over the network. Every command is signed. If a phone is already connected, any further device needs your approval on the laptop. Classmates can't take over.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no cloud. Everything stays between your phone and your laptop.

English, German, French, Spanish and Italian · light and dark theme · simple and pro mode.
SlimClick is free. Made by SlimBit Apps – small apps that do more and use less.
```

Referat oder Vortrag? Dein Handy ist der einzige Presenter, den du brauchst.

FUNKTIONIERT MIT JEDEM PRÄSENTATIONSPROGRAMM
