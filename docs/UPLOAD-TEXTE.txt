SPRECHTEXT (für Clipchamp)

Picture this. You're in front of the whole class, your slides are on the projector, and for every new slide you have to walk back to your laptop.

Or you ask a friend to click for you, and somehow they're always one slide behind.

That's why I built SlimClick. It turns your phone into a presentation remote, and you don't even have to install an app.

You open SlimClick on your laptop, scan the QR code with your phone's camera, and a web page opens. That's your remote.

Now you just tap next. It works with PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides and even PDFs.

Your phone also shows a live preview of the current slide, so you can face your audience and still know exactly where you are.

Want to point at something? Turn on the laser, touch the preview, and a red dot appears right there on the big screen. The spotlight darkens everything else.

There's a timer as well. A few minutes before the end, your phone screen flashes quietly, so you never run over time again.

And if your school Wi-Fi blocks the connection, SlimClick shows you step by step how to use your phone's hotspot instead.

It's also secure. The QR code only works once, every command is signed, and nobody else can take over your slides without your permission.

SlimClick is completely free for Windows and Mac, with no ads and no account. You'll find the link in the description. Good luck with your next presentation.

Your phone won't connect to your laptop at school? That's the school Wi-Fi, not you.

A lot of school networks keep devices apart, so your phone simply can't see your laptop.

The fix takes ten seconds. Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect your laptop to it.

SlimClick updates the QR code by itself. Scan it again, and you're back in control. It doesn't even use your mobile data.
