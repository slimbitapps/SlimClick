Forgot your clicker before a presentation? Your phone can do the job.

Open SlimClick on your laptop and scan the QR code with your camera. There's no app to install.

Now every tap on your phone moves your slides forward, in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides or a PDF.

You even get a live preview, so you always know which slide comes next.

SlimClick is free for Windows and Mac. The link is in my profile.
