Your phone won't connect to your laptop at school? That's the school Wi-Fi, not you.

A lot of school networks keep devices apart, so your phone simply can't see your laptop.

The fix takes ten seconds. Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect your laptop to it.

SlimClick updates the QR code by itself. Scan it again, and you're back in control. It doesn't even use your mobile data.
