Running out of time in the middle of your presentation is the worst.

SlimClick has a timer right on your phone. Pick ten, fifteen or twenty minutes and tap start.

A few minutes before the end, your phone screen quietly flashes yellow. Nobody in the room notices, but you know it's time to wrap up.

When the time is up, it flashes red, so you never run over again.

SlimClick is free for Windows and Mac. The link is in my profile.
