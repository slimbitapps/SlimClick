Worried a classmate could take over your slides? Here's why they can't.

The QR code in SlimClick only works once. As soon as your phone connects, the code changes, so a photo of it is useless.

Every tap you make is signed, so nobody else on the Wi-Fi can fake one.

And if another phone tries to join, your laptop asks you first. You decide.
